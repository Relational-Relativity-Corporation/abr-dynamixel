# Environment-region measurement declaration — DRAFT

**Status: OPEN.** Candidate sensor: RPLIDAR; exact model and installation unselected.

Potential observations: beam direction, reported range, return quality/intensity (only if supported by selected hardware), and sensor status. Their physical units, extents, synchronization, calibration, and provenance require Origin declaration and IRRS admission.

A LiDAR scan index does not establish a physical direction. A scan sample does not automatically establish a physical reflection-point locus or edge.

No LiDAR topology, coupling constants, thresholds, or operator outputs are authorized. Synthetic fixture outputs must be labeled SYNTHETIC and are not physical verification.
